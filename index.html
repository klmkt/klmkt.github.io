      About me

      I am a game developer and a fifth-semester Game Technology student at Politeknik Elektronika Negeri Surabaya (PENS). My experience covers game programming, technical art, and music production.

      My main focus is building game systems and mechanics, mostly with Unity. As a technical artist, I integrate 2D animations, sound effects, and user interfaces so that the playing experience feels cohesive and comfortable. I have also composed music for several games.

      I take part in game development competitions, which have trained me to work under deadlines and in multidisciplinary teams. I enjoy creating engaging gameplay and I’m always eager to learn new technologies.
